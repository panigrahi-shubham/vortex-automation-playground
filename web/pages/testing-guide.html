Manual testing · Six practical journeys

Test Vortex with confidence

Follow source-backed storefront and UI Lab walkthroughs, compare observable results, and record what actually happened. This guide does not sign you in, create fixtures, reset data, or change server settings.

Start with sign up Open UI Lab

Before you begin

Use an isolated local environment and disposable data that you own. A fresh account is not guaranteed to exist, and protected pages may ask you to sign in before continuing.

Use test data only

Do not enter real payment card details or expect real purchases, charges, refunds, or email delivery.

Keep your own records

Note the runtime, browser, viewport, theme, starting state, and actual outcome. Generated account and order IDs will vary.

Avoid shared resets

Do not use legacy global reset or chaos controls against a shared instance. This page provides no reset or server-control action.

Journeys

Run the journeys in order for a complete storefront pass, or use UI Lab independently. Record blocked work as blocked rather than treating an unexecuted step as a pass.

01

Account

Sign up and sign in

  • Start: /register
  • Needs: unique disposable account details

Steps

  1. Open Sign up, complete the required fields, and submit.
  2. Confirm that the new account reaches the storefront with a signed-in user menu.
  3. Sign out from the user menu, then open Sign in and use the same account.
  4. Reload the storefront and check whether the expected session remains active.

Expected outcome

  • Registration signs in the new account without exposing credentials.
  • Signing out removes access to protected pages until sign in succeeds again.
  • An invalid password produces an error and does not create an authenticated session.

Boundary: PostgreSQL MFA uses an authenticator app and may require a separate verification challenge. Legacy email OTP uses its Test Inbox instead. PostgreSQL recovery is available only in eligible non-production configurations and does not send real email.

02

Discovery

Filter the catalog

  • Start: /shop
  • Needs: no signed-in state for browsing

Steps

  1. Open the Shop and record the initial products and result count.
  2. Enter a distinctive search term and observe which products remain.
  3. Apply a category, stock, or sort control; change one control at a time and compare the results.
  4. Clear the filters, then open a product and confirm its name, price, stock, options, and details.

Expected outcome

  • Visible products and the result count agree with each applied filter.
  • Clearing filters restores the unfiltered catalog.
  • The product detail page corresponds to the selected product rather than its former row position.

Boundary: Search terms, stock, prices, and available variants depend on current data. Do not assume a generated product ID, a fixed result count, or that every product has options.

03

Saved items

Save to wishlist and build a cart

  • Start: /wishlist
  • Needs: your signed-in account and an available product

Steps

  1. From the Shop, save a product to the wishlist and open Wishlist.
  2. Confirm that the intended product appears once; remove and restore it if you need to test both states.
  3. Add an available product to the cart, selecting a valid size and colour first when options are required.
  4. Open Cart, change the quantity, review the totals, and reload.

Expected outcome

  • Wishlist and cart badges reflect the current saved items and quantities.
  • The cart retains the selected variant and recalculates line and order totals after quantity changes.
  • Reloading preserves the data for the active runtime and session.

Boundary: Legacy data survives only until its memory server restarts or resets. Stock and variant availability can block an add or quantity increase; record the error instead of forcing the control.

04

Purchase simulation

Checkout and verify an order

  • Start: /cart with items
  • Needs: your signed-in account

Steps

  1. Begin checkout from a non-empty Cart; an empty cart does not start the wizard.
  2. Complete Address, Delivery, Payment, and Review using disposable address data and a test card supplied by the Payment step.
  3. Before placing the order, compare item quantities, variants, discounts, shipping, tax, and total with the cart.
  4. Place the order once, follow the confirmation to Orders, and open the new user-owned order from the list.

Expected outcome

  • The new order retains the reviewed items, totals, address, delivery, and simulated payment summary.
  • The cart is cleared and the order remains visible after reload.
  • Repeated review clicks do not become evidence of duplicate orders; verify the actual Orders list.

Boundary: Payment is simulated: no real charge, refund, or order-confirmation email occurs. PostgreSQL does not offer the legacy 3-D Secure exercise; do not expect that challenge outside the legacy runtime.

05

Order lifecycle

Cancel an eligible order

  • Start: /orders
  • Needs: your own Placed or Processing order

Steps

  1. Open Orders while signed in and select an order owned by the current account.
  2. Confirm that its status is Placed or Processing, then choose Cancel order.
  3. Dismiss the confirmation once and verify that the status does not change.
  4. Choose Cancel order again, confirm it, and review the status and tracking timeline.

Expected outcome

  • Dismissing the dialog leaves the order unchanged.
  • Confirming changes the order to Cancelled and shows the Placed-to-Cancelled history.
  • The cancellation action disappears, and returned stock is reflected in the catalog.

Boundary: Shipped and Delivered orders cannot be cancelled through this journey. Any refund is simulated, not a real payment reversal. Use the Orders list rather than inventing or reusing an order ID.

06

Automation techniques

Explore UI Lab

  • Start: /lab
  • Needs: no storefront account for the Lab itself

Steps

  1. Open UI Lab and choose one independent card, such as dialogs, frames, form controls, waits, or downloads.
  2. Read the card title and technique hint before interacting with its visible controls.
  3. Perform the action and observe the card's own output, event, download, frame, or dialog result.
  4. Repeat with keyboard input where supported, then reset only that exercise through its provided control when available.

Expected outcome

  • Each exercise exposes an observable result within its own card or browser surface.
  • Nested-frame and open-shadow-root exercises remain bounded by their actual DOM structure.
  • Deliberately unstable or inaccessible examples affect only their designated exercise.

Boundary: Closed shadow-root internals are not promised to be selectable. Browser permissions can affect clipboard and geolocation exercises. The accessibility audit and visual-defect cards are intentionally faulty and are not quality baselines for the rest of the site.

Manual results checklist

Use these local checkboxes only after recording evidence elsewhere. They are not saved, submitted, or treated as deployment certification.

Quality checks for this guide

Keyboard and focus

Use Tab and Shift+Tab from the top. The skip link becomes visible on focus, all links and checkboxes are reachable, and focus indicators remain visible.

Responsive layout

Compare wide desktop and narrow mobile widths. Journey columns collapse, long content wraps, and the runtime table scrolls inside its labelled region rather than widening the page.

Light and dark themes

A test harness can apply the existing data-theme="dark" root hook to compare themes. This static page has no separate theme toggle.

Reduced motion

The shared styles honor the operating-system preference and the existing data-reduced-motion hook. This guide introduces no animation or smooth scrolling.

Android is separate work

The Android application is a separate API client, not part of this website. This guide supplies no APK and does not claim Appium coverage. Mobile API behavior, accessibility, and UI automation require their own implementation and verification.
